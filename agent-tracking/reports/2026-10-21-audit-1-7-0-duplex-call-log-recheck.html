Findings

D-1 | Medium | Docs state fields upstream does not define (hallucinated API)
swagger.yaml:3758-3760, docs/ASSISTANT-RULES.md:239-243, docs/CHANGELOG.md:14-15
All three say duplex usage carries voice_model, backend_model, estimated_cost_voice_usd, estimated_cost_backend_usd. A search of upstream's usage, webhook, call-logs and analytics pages finds none of these keys. Upstream documents the split inside model_usage, with a cost on each entry.
Why it matters: a client developer who reads our contract will code against keys that never arrive, and will miss the real ones. The README rules say never to guess an upstream field name.
Fix: replace the sentence with the real shape (see patch below). Whether the names came from an earlier upstream revision or were invented cannot be told from the repo; upstream as it reads now is the authority.
D-2 | Low | The actual 1.7.0 call-log change is not documented
swagger.yaml:3745-3760 (usage description), swagger.yaml:957-971 (call-log example has no model_usage or pricing fields), docs/ASSISTANT-RULES.md:237-243
Missing: model_usage[].type is voice_llm_usage / backend_llm_usage on new duplex calls and llm_usage on calls recorded before 1.7.0; each entry has its own estimated_cost_usd; llm_model is comma-separated for duplex; pricing_schema_version is 2 from 1.7.0.
Why it matters: upstream flags that a receiver matching type == "llm_usage" must accept the new names. Our consumers read our docs, not upstream's.
Fix: add the trap to the swagger description and to the webhook section of ASSISTANT-RULES.md, add a duplex model_usage example, and add one docs/CHANGELOG.md line.
D-3 | Low | call_end_reason list is incomplete
swagger.yaml:4667, docs/ASSISTANT-RULES.md:236
We list natural and max_duration_exceeded. Upstream's webhook page lists four: also end_call_tool and silence_timeout. Found while reading the webhook page; not a 1.7.0 change.
Why it matters: a strict client enum built from our docs rejects a real value.
Fix: list all four, say the field may be null on legacy records.
D-4 | Info | Structure conformance
Handlers delegate, forwarding goes through callExternal, no axios or process.env outside their layers in the files read. No violation.

Proposed doc patch (not applied)

swagger.yaml usage description, replace the duplex sentence with:

duplex calls (upstream 1.7.0) have two `model_usage` entries, typed `voice_llm_usage`
(GPT-Live, billed per session second) and `backend_llm_usage` (backend model, billed per
token), each with its own `estimated_cost_usd`; the call's `estimated_cost_usd` is their sum.
`llm_model` lists both, comma-separated. Calls recorded before 1.7.0 keep `llm_usage` on both
entries, so a receiver matching `type == "llm_usage"` must also accept the new names.
`pricing_schema_version` is 2 from 1.7.0 (service tiers priced). Forwarded unchanged:
accept unknown keys.

Same text, shortened, in docs/ASSISTANT-RULES.md lines 239-243. docs/CHANGELOG.md lines 14-15: correct the four field names to the model_usage shape.
